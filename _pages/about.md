I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Professor Junxian He in the HKUST NLP Group. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, and is concerned with large language models: how they can be made to reason better, and how their failures -- hallucinated content in vision-language models, or a drift away from truthfulness -- can be understood and mitigated.
