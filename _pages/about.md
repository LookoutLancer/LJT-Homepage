## About Me

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group, where I am supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, and focuses on making large language models and vision-language models more verifiable, truthful and interpretable.

I received my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. I worked with Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies there, and I was awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship at SJTU.

## Research Interests

- **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**
- **Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)**
- **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**

Research skills developed through my degree work and research experiences:
